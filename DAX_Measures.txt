Create these measures after importing blinkit_sales table:

Total Sales = SUM(blinkit_sales[Sales])
Average Sales = AVERAGE(blinkit_sales[Sales])
Number of Records = COUNTROWS(blinkit_sales)
Average Rating = AVERAGE(blinkit_sales[Rating])
Sales Share % = DIVIDE([Total Sales], CALCULATE([Total Sales], ALL(blinkit_sales)), 0)

Dashboard layout: KPI cards (Total Sales, Average Sales, Records, Average Rating);
bar chart Sales by Item Type; column chart Sales by Outlet Type; donut Sales by Fat Content;
column chart Sales by Location Tier; line chart Sales by Establishment Year; matrix by Outlet Type.
Slicers: Item Type, Outlet Type, Outlet Size, Location Tier, Fat Content.
