POWER BI BUILD GUIDE (Power BI Desktop required)
1. Get Data > Text/CSV > Dataset/blinkit_sales_sample.csv > Load.
2. Confirm column data types: year whole number, visibility decimal, weight/sales decimal, rating decimal.
3. Create measures from DAX_Measures.txt.
4. Add 4 Card visuals for KPI measures.
5. Add clustered bar: Item_Type axis, Total Sales values.
6. Add donut: Item_Fat_Content legend, Total Sales values.
7. Add column chart: Outlet_Location_Type axis, Total Sales values.
8. Add line chart: Outlet_Establishment_Year axis, Total Sales values.
9. Add table/matrix with Outlet_Type and measures.
10. Add slicers and format page with title “Blinkit Sales Analysis”. Save as Blinkit_Sales_Dashboard.pbix.
Note: PBIX cannot be generated/executed in this environment; this guide and dataset support its creation on a Windows PC.
